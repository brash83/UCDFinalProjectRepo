My Journey


Over the last six years working in RTÉ, I have significantly upskilled myself in various areas, including solutions architecture, system management, and web development. During this time, I’ve had the opportunity to lead and contribute to several impactful projects. I designed and implemented an overtime dashboard using Power BI, developed both a Recce Library app and a Vehicle Reservation app using Power Apps, and played a key role as the technical lead in the development and implementation of our 'Radio Booking System.'


These experiences have not only enhanced my technical expertise but also provided me with a deeper understanding of how to create solutions that drive business efficiency. The website you're currently visiting is a culmination of my recent work for the Front-End Web Development studio at UCD, showcasing my journey and growth as a web developer.
